# Studio Agent

A personal Android app that helps one artist get work into exhibitions: it protects studio time, documents the work,
writes the paperwork, finds and ranks open calls, builds application packs, runs slow outreach, and handles what comes
after a yes or a no. You make the art and tap approve.

Everything lives on the phone. There is no server. AI, email and GitHub features switch on when you add your own keys in Settings.

## Install

Every push to `main` builds a signed APK with GitHub Actions and publishes it under **Releases**.
Open the newest release on your phone, download the `.apk`, and install it. Later builds install over the old one.

## First run

1. A seven-page tour shows how the app works. Nothing has to be filled in; a name is optional.
2. Tap Start using it. That is all the setup there is. Allow notifications if you want quiet reminders.
3. Everything works without an AI key: drafts start from your own answers, with [brackets] for the line only you can write, and nothing can be sent with a bracket left in. An AI key, email and GitHub in More > Settings are optional extras.

## Look and feel

Gallery-wall white, graphite text, one cobalt for actions, and masking-tape yellow only for the one thing to do now.
Titles are set in Bricolage Grotesque, everything else in a copy of Atkinson Hyperlegible with a plain zero (renamed Studio Legible, as its licence asks).
Every build photographs the main screens in light, dark and large text and attaches them to the release as `screens.zip`.

## Where things are

Four tabs, the same every time. Each thing has one place.

- **Today**: the one next step, Start now, your week (tap it for the month calendar, studio times, pausing and session history), the next 7 days, the road to your first show, and anything waiting for your yes.
- **Studio**: your pieces, series and parked ideas.
- **Calls**: open calls this week, all of them (with a find box), and what you applied to as a board that follows each entry from chosen to the show and home again.
- **You**: Everything the app can do; Find anything (or ask a question);  Statement, bio and CV; People and venues; Guide (with the five levels); Records (to-dos, money, sales, proof of what was sent); Settings; Help (the tour, plain answers, about).

## Everything else

On You, **Everything the app can do** lists every tool, grouped by what you want to get done:

- **Making the work**: sessions (with a studio friend to text when you start), Empty your head (everything at once, sorted into jobs, things to buy and ideas), Foundations (twelve weeks of basics, six on building a body of work, six going further, and a master to study from a museum's open collection), Fresh eyes (flip, no color, squint, values, thirds, colors, compare with the photo before, questions to look again with), planning a series, photographing every piece in one go, supplies (with what they cost, into Records).
- **Showing it**: open calls with distance and your themes in the ranking, past answers offered again on new entry forms, no's that count toward the year's sends, calls you entered remembered for next year, packing for drop-off day, Your people (life drawing, sketch groups, art leagues), an entry prepared ahead for strong free calls (never sent without you), entries in 15-minute evenings, places near you, venue history, studio visits, group shows, announcements for local listings, shipping, labels, slides for a talk, an online show in one file.
- **Selling it**: prices and raising them, commissions from quote to hand-over, selling at an opening, Market day with price tags and postcards to print, teaching a beginner class (outline, supplies, fee, the email, and a CV line after), buyers thanked and kept in touch with.
- **Being seen**: what jurors find when they search your name. Social media help is off unless you turn it on.
- **Money and records**: receipts by photo, art trips by the mile, the year month by month and in one picture, insurance values, a tax page.

Settings, Hours and calm has days off from the app, Calm Today, Wi-Fi-only checks, and the social media switch. In the menu on Today's step: "It is all too much" (three quiet days: one five-minute step, no notifications), "Remind me this evening", and "Empty my head first". Your week takes an off day or an extra shift for just this week. Firsts (first piece, first yes, first sale) are noticed once, on the day.

## Quick ways in

- Long-press the icon: Start now, Calls, Park an idea, Empty your head.
- The Start now widget and quick-settings tile start a session in one tap. The Next step widget shows today's step on the home screen.
- Share a link, text or flyer into the app to add a call, or photograph a flyer from Calls: its words are read on the phone (offline, no key). Share several pictures from the gallery to add them as pieces.

## Works with nothing set up

- Calls: eight public listing sites (CaFÉ, ArtCall, Artwork Archive, Art Deadline, ArtConnect, ArtRabbit, ArtJobs, Artshow) are read once a day without AI, and only where the site's robots.txt allows it. Each new call's own page is opened and read for title, deadline, fee, kind and scope.
- Writing help: a free public service that needs no account (Pollinations, with LLM7 as the fallback). If it doesn't answer, every button falls back to a starter built from your own words. A key of your own, from any provider, can be pasted in; the app recognises the provider from the key.
- Help from the writing help, each with a plain fallback: "In plain words" on every call, "Suggest small steps" on a piece, and "Ask anything" in Help.
- Email: type your address and the servers fill themselves in for Gmail, Yahoo, iCloud, AOL, Fastmail and Zoho. Without email set up, each application opens in your mail app, filled in.
- Places: real galleries, art centers, libraries, print labs and framers near you, from OpenStreetMap (no key). Venues' own sites are read for exhibition pages and upcoming openings.
- Shows of your own: a venue's yes becomes dated steps, a flyer with a QR code, labels, an agreement to sign, photos of the show, and what to say at the opening.
- Documents: CV, portfolio, catalogue of works, price list, wall labels, flyer, sign-up sheet, agreement, certificate, bill of sale and proposal (with the series drawn on a wall at real size), and all materials as one zip, set in the app's typefaces, with titles of works in italics, sizes as h × w in with cm, catalog numbers, and Lastname_What.pdf names. Exported pictures carry your name and a copyright line inside the file.
